## 📝 Problem Statement

You are given an integer array 
nums
.




The 
low
 score of 
nums
 is the 
minimum
 absolute difference between any two integers.


The 
high
 score of 
nums
 is the 
maximum
 absolute difference between any two integers.


The 
score
 of 
nums
 is the sum of the 
high
 and 
low
 scores.




Return the 
minimum score
 after 
changing two elements
 of 
nums
.
